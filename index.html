How to use CPT Plot Digitizer

Extract digital data from a CPT plot image in 7 steps. Everything runs in your browser.

1
📷 Upload image
Upload a PNG or JPG of your CPT plot. The image is never sent anywhere.
2
🎯 Calibrate axes
Place two known points. Each uses two clicks: first sets X position, second sets depth. Enter the real values and click Apply.
3
⬜ Mark plot area
Click two diagonal corners bounding the curve area.
4
🪄 Auto-trace
Pick the curve color (or Auto-dark), adjust sliders, hit Run.
5
✏️ Refine
Move: drag a teal dot. Add: click empty space. Delete: click a dot. Region: drag a box to re-trace that area.
6
👁 Verify
Toggle "Show overlay" to see resampled data as a red line over the image. Click table cells to override values.
7
⬇ Export
Download CSV or copy to clipboard.

Tips

• Zoom in when placing calibration points for accuracy.

• Pick calibration points far apart for best results.

• If the tracer catches gridlines, lower tolerance and use Refine Region on problem areas.
